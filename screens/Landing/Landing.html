What MoMents do we bring

Everything you need for a supported, memorable pregnancy journey

Monthly Baby Growth Projection

See your baby's development through monthly 2D visuals, paired with relatable size comparisons and AI-guided recommendations for every stage.

Health Tracking & AI Insights

Easily monitor your vitals, sync Fitbit data, and receive personalized health suggestions to stay proactive and prepared.

Journey Journal & Memory Keeper

Capture daily emotions, special milestones, and unforgettable moments with photos, notes, and a digital scrapbook you can cherish forever.

Share with Friends & Family

Invite loved ones to follow your journey through private access, so they stay connected no matter the distance.

AI Chat Support, Anytime

Our medically trained chatbot offers guidance, symptom support, and a comforting voice whenever you need it most.
